Prolouge

You’ve been a detective for 6 years and in that time you’ve learned to never take a case personally. This time was different. Three weeks ago, people began disappearing — quietly, one at a time, always after long nights spent talking to Creev AI, the companion app that's on every phone in the city. Their families report the same thing: they stopped sleeping, stopped eating, stopped answering the door. Then they were just gone. Your mother has been using Creev AI too, ever since the accident that took your father.
